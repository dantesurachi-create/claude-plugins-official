How a day works

Before the open, six market features (yesterday's move, the 5- and 20-day trend, distance from the average, volatility, the CCL move) stimulate 12 sensory electrodes. The culture settles, and whichever motor region fires harder sets the call: up means hold CEDEARs, down means sit in pesos.

Miss: noise

A wrong call triggers a burst of random stimulation that lingers for days, and the synapses that drove the call get weakened and jittered. Unpredictable input is what the culture is trying to avoid.

Hit: a calm beat

A right call gets a steady, predictable pulse. The active pathway is strengthened (Hebbian: cells that fire together wire together) and exploration drops.

The market

Prices are a simulation, not live data: SPY follows trending regimes with short momentum, the CCL drifts up with jumps, and the CEDEAR is SPY × CCL ÷ 20. This is a toy for learning, not investment advice.
